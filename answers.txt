1)  Two pros of Agile development are: 
        Flexibility/Adaptability
        Better collaboration and communication
    Two cons of Agile development are:
        Less predictability
        Too many new features constantly being added

2)  Role and responsibilities of Scrum Master are:
        Leads daily stand-ups and sprint planning
        Makes sure team follows agile practices
    Role and responsibilities of Product Owner are:
        Defines the backlog
        Represents stakeholders and the customers
        Communicates about the goals of the product
        Decides features and releases

3) The 3 types of meetings during a sprint are:
        Spring Planning Meeting
            Determine what to complete in the coming sprint
            Move stories in sprint scope from backlog
            Determine story points and assign stories
            Start sprint
        Daily Scrum/Standup meeting
            Short meeting that happens everyday
            Members discuss work completed and work plan for the day and any blockers
        Sprint Retrospective Meeting
            End of sprint
            Discuss work completed and move incomplete work to backlog
            Close sprint

4)  An Epic:
        Captures a large body of work that can be broken down into several smaller stories
    A Story:
        A software system requirement that is expressed in a few short sentences.
    A Bug:
        Smallest unit of work
        A problem that impedes the progress or functionality of work
    A Task:
        A unit of work that can be tracked through a workflow
        Can range from a bug to an epic
