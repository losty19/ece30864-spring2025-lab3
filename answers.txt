1)  Two pros of Agile development are: 
        Flexibility/Adaptability
        Better collaboration and communication
    Two cons of Agile development are:
        Less predictability
        Too many new features constantly being added

2)  Role and responsibilities of Scrum Master are:
        Leads daily stand-ups and sprint planning
        Makes sure team follows agile practices
    Role and responsibilities of Product Owner are:
        Defines the backlog
        Represents stakeholders and the customers
        Communicates about the goals of the product
        Decides features and releases
